Overview:-
This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze mobile sales performance across different brands, cities, customer segments, and payment methods. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights, enabling data-driven decision-making through dynamic visualizations and KPI tracking.

Objectives
Analyze overall sales performance and revenue trends.
Track brand-wise and model-wise sales distribution.
Understand customer demographics and purchasing behavior.
Compare sales performance across different cities.
Evaluate payment method preferences.
Monitor key business KPIs for strategic decision-making.
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
💰 Total Sales
📦 Total Quantity Sold
🔄 Total Transactions
📈 Average Sales
Dashboard Features
Interactive filters and slicers
Brand-wise sales analysis
City-wise performance comparison
Customer age and rating insights
Payment method distribution
Daily and monthly sales trends
Dynamic KPI cards and visual reports
